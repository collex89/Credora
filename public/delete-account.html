Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Crescamus account and everything tied to it, whether or not you still have the app installed.

If you have the app

InstantDelete from inside the app

  1. Open Crescamus and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  3. Type DELETE to confirm

Your account and data are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app

By requestEmail us to request deletion

Send an email to crescamus.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." Include your username if you remember it.

Email crescamus.app@gmail.com

We'll verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Profileusername, name, parish, bio, photo
  • Posts, comments & likeseverything you've posted
  • Follows & followersyour connections to other users
  • Direct messagessent and received
  • Prayer intentionsincluding completion history
  • Account credentialsemail and encrypted password

Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Once processed, this data cannot be recovered by you or by us.

See our Privacy Policy for how this data was used while your account was active. Questions: crescamus.app@gmail.com
